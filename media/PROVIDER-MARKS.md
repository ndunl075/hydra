# Provider marks

`webview/ProviderLogo.tsx` embeds the unchanged path geometry of these official marks.
Their fill inherits the node foreground for native theme contrast. The marks
identify the provider assigned to a task; they do not imply endorsement.

- **Claude:** `resources/claude-logo.svg` from the installed
  `anthropic.claude-code` extension, version `2.1.274` (Anthropic).
  Extension notice: © Anthropic PBC. All rights reserved. Use is subject to
  [Claude Code legal agreements](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance).
- **OpenAI:** `resources/blossom-black.svg` from the installed
  `openai.chatgpt` extension, version `26.721.30844` (OpenAI).
  The extension's `LICENSE.md` refers to
  [OpenAI terms of use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use).

These brand assets retain their respective owners' rights; they are not relicensed
